title: "God Rays"
description: "Animated rays of light radiating from the center, blended with up to 5 colors. The rays are adjustable by size, density, brightness and center glow. Great for dramatic backgrounds, logo reveals, and VFX like energy bursts or sun shafts."
---

Animated rays of light radiate outward from a central point, with each color layer independently rotated to build depth. A central glow shape adds a focal bloom, and a bloom/overlay color composites additively over the rays. Great for dramatic backgrounds, logo reveals, and VFX like energy bursts or sun shafts.

## Props

<ParamField path="colorBack" type="string" default="#000000">
  Background color behind the rays.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="colorBloom" type="string" default="#0000ff">
  Overlay color blended additively with the rays when `bloom` is greater than `0`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="colors" type="string[]" default='["#a600ff6e","#6200fff0","#ffffff","#33fff5"]'>
  Array of up to 5 ray colors. Each color is applied to an independent ray layer.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="density" type="number" default="0.3">
  Number/density of rays. Range: `0` to `1`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="spotty" type="number" default="0.3">
  Length of the rays. Higher values produce shorter, more spot-like rays. Range: `0` to `1`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="intensity" type="number" default="0.8">
  Visibility/brightness of the rays. Range: `0` to `1`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="midSize" type="number" default="0.2">
  Size of the circular glow at the center. Range: `0` to `1`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="midIntensity" type="number" default="0.4">
  Brightness of the central glow. Range: `0` to `1`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="bloom" type="number" default="0.4">
  Blend between alpha compositing (`0`) and additive blending (`1`) for the ray colors. Range: `0` to `1`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="speed" type="number" default="0.75">
  Animation speed multiplier. Set to `0` to pause.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="frame" type="number" default="0">
  Static frame position when `speed` is `0`.
</ParamField>

## Presets

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `Default` | Purple and cyan rays from below center |
| `Warp` | Pink and orange rays with blue bloom |
| `Linear` | Subtle white rays from upper right |
| `Ether` | Sparse, spotty blue rays on dark background |
